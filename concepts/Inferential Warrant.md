# Inferential Warrant (`C-11`)

## Formulation

`Inferential Warrant` is the epistemic entitlement or justification generated or transmitted for a conclusion belief through purportedly deductive reasoning from warranted premise beliefs.

## Explanation

When you start from justified premises and deduce a conclusion, *inferential warrant* is the epistemic justification that the reasoning step supplies or passes along to your conclusion. Philosophers fight over this concept because unnoticed slow switching under [[Content Externalism]] produces a troubling case: a thinker reasons carefully and competently from her own perspective, yet her inference is technically invalid because two occurrences of the same word secretly carry different world-dependent meanings. The core disagreement is whether deductive reasoning can still give you justification when you exercise a normally reliable reasoning skill in an abnormal environment, whether actual logical validity is an absolute requirement, or whether mere blamelessness would be too weak a standard.

### Candidate Sharpenings
- [[Unrestricted Validity Requirement Definition]] (`D-37`) — Deductive reasoning yields inferential warrant only if the specific reasoning step is actually logically valid, with no exceptions for hidden externalist equivocation.
- [[Competence Validity-Conduciveness Warrant Definition]] (`D-38`) — Deductive reasoning yields inferential warrant whenever the thinker exercises a fallible reasoning skill that reliably produces valid inferences in normal environments, even if an abnormal switch makes one instance invalid.
- [[Deontological Blamelessness Warrant Definition]] (`D-39`) — Deductive reasoning yields inferential warrant whenever the thinker is simply cognitively blameless and doing the best she can, regardless of whether her reasoning habit is reliably truth- or validity-conducive.

## Usage in Literature

[[Mikkel Gerken]] brings `Inferential Warrant` to center stage by formulating the *Validity Argument* against anti-individualism without relying on any transparency premise ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning]]; Gerken Validity Argument Against Anti-Individualism ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning#Arguments]])). Given two-concept externalism (`D-21`), Peter's slow-switched deduction equivocates (`P-47`) even though it intuitively generates conditional warrant (`P-48`); combining those verdicts with the Unrestricted Validity Requirement (`D-37`, Unrestricted Validity Requirement on Inferential Warrant ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning#Propositions]])) yields a reductio of anti-individualism. Gerken resolves the inconsistent quintet by rejecting `D-37` in favor of competence-based validity-conduciveness (`D-38`): because reasoning competencies—such as univocal middle-term redeployment—are fallible yet reliably validity-conducive in normal environments, their optimal exercise still confers conditional warrant in abnormal slow-switch environments (Competence-Based Conditional Warrant Without Token Validity ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning#Propositions]])).

At the same time, Gerken explicitly wards off the charge of epistemic deontologism (`D-39`) using Pete's blameless gambler's fallacy (Gambler Fallacy Refutation of Epistemic Deontologism ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning#Arguments]])): Pete reasons blamelessly yet lacks inferential warrant because he exercises an epistemically defective habit rather than a validity-conducive competence ([[Gerken 2011 - Conceptual Equivocation and Warrant by Reasoning]]). Complementing this picture, [[Paulo Faria]] examines unsafe reasoning (such as the Harry and Barry Golden Retrievers case) and draws on the Campbell-Carroll regress to show that empirical co-reference conditions enabling valid inference function as background presuppositions rather than extra premises ([[Faria 2009 - Unsafe reasoning A survey]]; [[Empirical Coreference Presuppositions Are Not Additional Premises]]), aligning with externalist accounts that tie warrant to reliable normal environments rather than guaranteed token validity.
