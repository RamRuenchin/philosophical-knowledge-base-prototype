# Peter Case Is Theory-Neutral Across Content Theories (`P-58`)

## Formulation

Slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism.

## Explanation

In Boghossian's slow-switching puzzle (`P-46`), Peter is unknowingly transported from Earth to Twin Earth and performs an apparently valid syllogism combining an Earth memory (about Earth Pavarotti or water) with a Twin-Earth perception (about Twin Pavarotti or "twater"). Rather than being a problem unique to [[Content Externalism]] (the view that mental contents depend partly on the external environment), this thesis holds that the puzzle is theory-neutral: both externalists and content internalists (who hold that mental contents depend solely on what is inside the head) must account for Peter's thoughts and his intuitive rationality.

## Usage in Literature

[[Manuel Pérez Otero]] establishes `P-58` in [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]] as the minimal thesis of `ARG-07`, systematically surveying five same-content and one different-content internalist strategies to show that internalists face severe semantic, ontological, or epistemological trade-offs on the very same case. In [[Boghossian 2014 - Reply to Otero's Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]], [[Paul Boghossian]] explicitly concedes `P-58`—agreeing that transparency failures pose a general challenge across theories of content—while blocking Otero's stronger comparative reversal (`P-57`) by appealing to narrow syntactic validity (`P-61`).
