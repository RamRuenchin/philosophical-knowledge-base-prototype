# Same-Content Internalist Accounts Strain Semantic Intuitions (`P-59`)

## Formulation

Same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions.

## Explanation

One way for a content internalist (who holds that mental content is fixed entirely inside the head) to handle slow-switched Peter (`P-46`) is to assign the exact same content to Peter's pre-switch Earth thoughts and post-switch Twin-Earth thoughts—for instance, by saying both tokens of "Pavarotti" refer to a single amalgam, to a disjunctive pair of singers, or via an indexical rule (shifting reference with context like "this" or "here"). This thesis claims that every such same-content internalist account strains ordinary semantic and ontological intuitions, because it either conflates two distinct people (Earth Pavarotti and Twin Pavarotti) or forces an ordinary proper name to behave like a cumbersome disjunction or indexical.

## Usage in Literature

[[Manuel Pérez Otero]] endorses `P-59` in [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]] as a central premise of `ARG-07`, arguing across five same-content internalist models of Peter's "Pavarotti" tokens—unified reference, disjunctive amalgam reference, and three indexical models—that each either identifies two distinct singers, treats a singular proper name as disjunctive or indexical, or inherits the objections to externalist anaphoric solutions while adding indexical implausibility. In [[Boghossian 2014 - Reply to Otero's Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]], [[Paul Boghossian]] does not defend same-content internalism for proper names, instead responding that his transparency challenge applies equally to general terms like "water" and turning to narrow syntactic validity (`P-61`) to block `ARG-07`.
