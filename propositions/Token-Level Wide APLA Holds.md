# Token-Level Wide APLA Holds (`P-21`)

## Formulation

Every thinker can know a priori the logical validity or invalidity of every concrete wide-content inference that thinker performs.

## Explanation

This thesis sharpens [[Thinkers Possess A Priori Logical Abilities]] (`P-03`) under [[Token-Level Wide APLA Definition]] (`D-09`), where `APLA` stands for *a priori logical abilities*. Rather than claiming merely that you understand abstract logical rules like *modus ponens* (`P-22`), it makes the far stronger claim that purely from reflection—without checking empirical facts—you can tell whether any *specific, concrete inference* you actually make is logically valid or secretly equivocates, even when your thoughts' meanings are "wide" (partly fixed by the external environment). Paul Boghossian argues that slow-switched Peter refutes this thesis under externalism: having unknowingly moved from Earth to Twin Earth, Peter combines a pre-switch Earth memory about Pavarotti with a post-switch perception of Twin Pavarotti in an inference that looks valid from inside but actually equivocates between two different people.

## Inferential Relations
### Leads To
- Structurally entails [[Form-Level APLA Holds]] (`P-22`).
- Its negation (`~P-21`) motivates adopting [[Externalism Is Compatible with Logical-Luck Rationality]] (`P-28`) while preserving [[Form-Level APLA Holds]] (`P-22`) ([[Faria 2009 - Unsafe reasoning A survey]], [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]]).

### Led To By / Challenged By
- Defeasibly challenged by [[Empirical Coreference Presuppositions Are Not Additional Premises]] (`P-103`) and by ordinary demonstrative-error cases (e.g., Faria's Golden Retrievers Harry and Barry), which show concrete inferential validity depends on contingent empirical coreference presuppositions rather than pure a priori reflection ([[Faria 2009 - Unsafe reasoning A survey]], [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]]).

## Usage in Literature

In [[Boghossian 1992 - Externalism and Inference]] and [[Boghossian 1994 - The Transparency of Mental Content]], [[Paul Boghossian]] treats this concrete token-level reading of APLA as the traditional rationalist principle threatened by content externalism: in [[Slow-Switch Equivocation vs. Token-Wide APLA]] (`ARG-03`), undetectable equivocation in Peter's slow-switching inference (`P-46`, `P-47`) is logically inconsistent with Token-Level Wide APLA (`P-21`). In [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]], [[Manuel Pérez Otero]] highlights how unusually demanding this token-level thesis is relative to [[Form-Level APLA Holds]] (`P-22`), noting that externalists who accept logical luck can reject `P-21` without surrendering a priori mastery of abstract logical forms. [[Paulo Faria]] likewise rejects `P-21` in [[Faria 2009 - Unsafe reasoning A survey]], arguing via ordinary demonstrative-error cases (such as the Golden Retrievers Harry and Barry) and `P-103` that concrete inferential validity depends on contingent empirical coreference presuppositions.
