# Full Comparative Epistemic Transparency Holds (`P-12`)

## Formulation

Both every thinker can know a priori, for every two same-content thought tokens of that thinker, that they have the same content, and every thinker can know a priori, for every two different-content thought tokens of that thinker, that they differ in content.

## Explanation

Often called *authoritative discriminative self-knowledge* (`DASK`), this conjunctive theory sharpens [[Mental Content Is Epistemically Transparent]] (`P-02`) under [[Comparative A Priori Transparency Definition]] (`D-01`) by demanding complete inward visibility of how your thoughts compare in meaning. In plain terms, it claims that purely by reflecting—without checking the external world—you can always tell both when two of your thoughts mean the exact same thing (transparency of sameness of content, `TSC`) and when they mean different things (transparency of difference of content, `TDC`). Because it combines both requirements, any counterexample to either sameness or difference (such as slow switching or unrecognized co-reference) refutes the full package.

### Conjuncts
- [[Transparency of Sameness of Content]] (`P-10`)
- [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`)

## Inferential Relations
### Leads To
- Structurally entails [[Transparency of Sameness of Content]] (`P-10`) and [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`).
- Its negation (`~P-12`) structurally entails the negation of [[Strong Meaning Rationalism Holds]] (`P-20`).

### Led To By / Challenged By
- Structurally entailed by the stronger [[Strong Meaning Rationalism Holds]] (`P-20`).
- Directly refuted by the negation of either [[Transparency of Sameness of Content]] (`P-10`) or [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`).

## Usage in Literature

[[Jessica Brown]] ([[Brown 2004 - Anti-Individualism and Knowledge]]) and [[John M. Collins]] ([[Collins 2008 - Content Externalism and Brute Logical Error]]) both reject `P-12`, accepting that content externalism defeats comparative epistemic transparency and requires revising traditional logical norms rather than abandoning externalism. [[Paul Boghossian]] in [[Boghossian 1994 - The Transparency of Mental Content]] and [[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited]] uses the externalist failure of `P-12` (`~P-12`) together with [[Rational Inference Requires Comparative A Priori Transparency]] (`P-13`) in `ARG-05` to argue that [[Wide Content Alone Suffices for Rationalizing Explanation]] (`P-54`) collapses. Because `P-12` is a conjunction of `P-10` and `P-11`, it is also defeated by independent single-paper arguments against either conjunct, including Morvarid's Oscar reliability argument against `P-10` ([[Morvarid 2021 - A new argument for the incompatibility of content externalism with justification internalism#Arguments]]) and Brown's Frances element case against `P-11` ([[Brown 2004 - Anti-Individualism and Knowledge#Arguments]]).
