# Speaker-Weighting Metasemantic Contextualism Holds (`P-32`)

## Formulation

A thinker's mental contents are assigned context-dependent semantic values by weighted interpretive charity governed by the thinker's own commitment priorities.

## Explanation

This thesis directly sharpens [[Metasemantic Contextualism Adequately Explains Mental Content]] (`P-06`), capturing Henry Jackman's distinctive brand of [[Metasemantic Contextualism]] (`S-03`). Rather than letting an outside interpreter's changing goals dictate what you mean (`P-33`) or locking each memory permanently to the planet where it was first encoded (`P-34`), it says what your concepts refer to in a given deliberative context is fixed by the relative weights of *your own* commitments—following the Self-Interpretation Principle. Because slow-switched Peter's brief, introspectively surveyable inference (mixing a pre-switch Earth memory with a post-switch Twin Earth perception) happens inside a single deliberative context with uniform commitment weights, Jackman argues that Peter's repeated terms stay locked to one meaning throughout, preventing equivocation (`P-47`).

## Inferential Relations
### Leads To
- Directly opposes [[Source-Dependent Contextualism Holds]] (`P-34`), replacing per-token historical source fixation with holistic speaker-commitment weighting across a deliberative context ([[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge]]).

### Led To By / Challenged By
- Directly opposed by [[Source-Dependent Contextualism Holds]] (`P-34`) and defeasibly challenged by [[Intentional Realism]] (`P-42`) ([[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited]], [[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge]]).

## Usage in Literature

In [[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge]], [[Henry Jackman]] endorses this view to unify conflicting intuitions about Burge's arthritis, Putnam's Twin Earth, and Donnellan/Evans switching cases, and deploys it in Jackman Single-Context Refutation of Peter Equivocation ([[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge#Arguments]]) to argue that because Peter's introspectively surveyable inference occupies a single deliberative context, uniform commitment weights force univocality and block equivocation (`P-47`) without premise-order sensitivity. [[Paul Boghossian]] rejects this contextualist strategy, maintaining Intentional Realism (`P-42`) and originary memory fixation against contextualist reallocations of content, and content-shifting contextualist replies also face pressure from Goldberg's memorial justification dilemma ([[Goldberg-2007-Anti-Individualism, Content Preservation, and Discursive Justification#Arguments]]).
