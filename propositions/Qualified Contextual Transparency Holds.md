# Qualified Contextual Transparency Holds (`P-18`)

## Formulation

Mental content is epistemically transparent relative to the thinker's tacit contextual presuppositions and an interpreter's contextual attribution.

## Explanation

Sharpening [[Mental Content Is Epistemically Transparent]] (`P-02`) under [[Qualified Contextual Transparency Definition]] (`D-03`), this thesis—associated with Robert Stalnaker—says that thoughts remain transparent not because you gaze directly at inner mental objects, but because an interpreter attributes beliefs in light of your unspoken contextual background assumptions. For example, in Stalnaker's misidentified-ship case (mistaking one ship for another) or slow-switched Peter's case (unknowingly moving from Earth to Twin Earth and mixing a pre-switch memory about Earth water or Pavarotti with a post-switch perception of Twin Earth twater or Twin Pavarotti), attributing the thinker's tacit background assumption that the two things are identical makes their reasoning come out valid and transparent relative to that context.

## Usage in Literature

[[Robert Stalnaker]] (reconstructed in [[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited]] and [[Schroeter 2013 - Are Concepts Creatures of Darkness]]) endorses `P-18` alongside radical contextualism (`P-33`) and tacit identity presuppositions (Tacit Identity Presupposition Restores Validity in Ship and Peter Cases ([[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited#Propositions]])) to reconcile anti-individualism with transparency. [[Paul Boghossian]] rejects `P-18` in [[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited]], arguing that Stalnaker's presupposition-attribution criterion is circular (Circularity of Stalnaker Tacit Presupposition Reply ([[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited#Arguments]])) because determining what Peter would rationally accept already presupposes settling whether his original inference commits a simple logical error.
