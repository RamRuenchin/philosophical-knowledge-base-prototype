# Non-Millian Externalism Matches Internalist Differentiators (`P-60`)

## Formulation

Non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism.

## Explanation

"Millianism" is the strict view that a term's meaning is exhausted by the external object it refers to; *non-Millian* [[Content Externalism]] rejects that restriction and allows mental contents to include internal cognitive modes of presentation in addition to external environmental determinants. This thesis claims that whenever content internalists specify a workable internal mental feature that differentiates slow-switched Peter's pre-switch and post-switch thoughts (`P-46`), a non-Millian externalist can adopt that exact same internal differentiator without giving up externalism.

## Usage in Literature

[[Manuel Pérez Otero]] endorses `P-60` in [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]] as a key symmetry premise in `ARG-07`. Because standard [[Content Externalism]] does not claim that external factors exhaust mental content (only radical Millianism equates meaning solely with reference), Otero argues that if internalism succeeds in specifying introspectively accessible internal factors that distinguish Peter's two "Pavarotti" tokens, a mixed non-Millian externalist (adopting *Different-Internally-and-Externally-Determined-Meaning*) can adopt the very same internal differentiators without abandoning external reference determination.
