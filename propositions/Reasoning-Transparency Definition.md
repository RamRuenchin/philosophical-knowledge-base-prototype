# Reasoning-Transparency Definition (`D-08`)

## Formulation
Transparency of mental content denotes purely formal, non-material transparency of reasoning transitions independent of external material individuation.

## Explanation
This definition treats valid reasoning as completely determined by introspectively accessible logical form alone, leaving no role for external environmental or social-material facts in fixing what inference a thinker is making. Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira isolates this formalist assumption in order to attack it from global content externalism, arguing that because the contents and transitions of thought are partly fixed by the external world, reasoning is individuated materially as well as formally. Under this reading, [[Rational Inference Requires Transparency of Mental Content]] (`P-01`) sharpens into [[Rational Inference Requires Reasoning-Transparency]] (`P-16`).

**Defines:** [[Transparency of Mental Content]] (`C-01`)

## Usage in Literature

[[Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira]] formulates this sharpening as the target "reasoning-transparency" thesis in [[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons]], using global content externalism to derive the material individuation of reasoning and thereby refute `P-16`.
