# Bridge - Undetectable Equivocation Defeats TDC (`P-64`)

## Formulation

If both (possibly, a slow-switched thinker performs a subjectively valid syllogism combining a pre-switch memory premise with a post-switch experiential premise) and (if a slow-switched thinker performs a subjectively valid syllogism combining a pre-switch memory premise with a post-switch experiential premise, then that thinker commits an introspectively undetectable equivocation between distinct wide contents), then it is not the case that every thinker can know a priori, for every two different-content thought tokens of that thinker, that they differ in content.

## Explanation

*Transparency of Difference of Content* (TDC, `P-11`) is the thesis that whenever two of a thinker's thought tokens differ in content, the thinker can know *a priori* (by reflection alone, without empirical investigation) that they differ. This bridge states that if Peter's slow-switching scenario is possible (`P-46`) and produces an introspectively undetectable equivocation between two distinct environmentally determined ("wide") contents (`P-47`)—as when Peter unknowingly mixes an Earth memory of water or Pavarotti with a Twin-Earth perception of "twater" or Twin Pavarotti—then TDC is directly refuted by counterexample.
