# Externalism Is Compatible with Dual Social-Material Rationality (`P-29`)

## Formulation

Global content externalism is compatible with inferential rationality when internal epistemic blamelessness is separated from socially and materially individuated reasoning.

## Explanation

This thesis sharpens [[Content Externalism Is Compatible with Rational Inference]] (`P-04`) under [[Dual Internal-Blamelessness and Social-Material Rationality Definition]] (`D-17`). It reconciles global content externalism—the view that nearly all mental contents and reasoning practices depend on the external environment and linguistic community—with rationality by splitting inferential evaluation into two distinct tiers: subjective epistemic blamelessness and abstract formal skills stay "inside the head," whereas which concrete inference you are actually performing and whether it is objectively valid are fixed by external physical and social relations. Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira uses this two-tier picture to absorb both slow-switch equivocations (Peter unknowingly moving between Earth and Twin Earth) and Kripke's Paderewski puzzles (a thinker holding two unrecognized guises for one man) without calling the blameless thinker irrational.

## Usage in Literature

In [[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons]], [[Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira]] endorses this dual framework as a radicalized externalist incompatibilism ("bite the bullet and swallow it too"). After deriving material individuation of reasoning from global externalism in Global Externalism to Material Individuation of Reasoning ([[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons#Arguments]]) and rejecting reasoning-transparency (`P-16`), he argues that slow-switch equivocation and Paderewski contradictions cease to be scandalous once subjective blamelessness is cleanly separated from social-material validity.
