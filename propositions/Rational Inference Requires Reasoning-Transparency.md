# Rational Inference Requires Reasoning-Transparency (`P-16`)

## Formulation

Every rational inference is individuated purely formally by logical form, independent of the reasoner's relations to the environment and speech community.

## Explanation

Sharpening [[Rational Inference Requires Transparency of Mental Content]] (`P-01`) under [[Reasoning-Transparency Definition]] (`D-08`), this thesis claims that what makes a piece of reasoning the specific inference it is depends *purely* on its internal logical structure—not on the reasoner's physical surroundings or language community—so a thinker can always know purely from inside which inference they are carrying out. Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira rejects this view: in slow-switching cases where Peter unknowingly moves from Earth to Twin Earth and mixes an Earth memory with a Twin Earth perception, Peter cannot tell from introspection alone whether he is performing an Earthian or Twin-Earthian inference, because under global externalism the environment and community partly determine what reasoning he is actually executing.

## Usage in Literature

In [[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons]], [[Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira]] rejects `P-16` as a radicalization of externalist incompatibilism. He argues that even Burge's anaphoric memory solution leaves slow-switched Peter blind to whether he is performing a sound Earthian inference or an unsound Twin-Earthian inference, and derives from Global Content Externalism ([[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons#Propositions]]) that reasoning is both formally and materially individuated ([[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons#Arguments]]), directly falsifying `P-16`.
