# Otero Comparative Reversal vs. Boghossian Narrow-Syntax Reply (`ARG-07`)

## Formulation

- **Inconsistent literal set:** `¬ (P-58 ∧ P-59 ∧ P-60 ∧ ¬P-61 ∧ P-67 ∧ ¬P-57)`

- **Asserted (`P-58`):** [[Peter Case Is Theory-Neutral Across Content Theories]] — Slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism.
- **Asserted (`P-59`):** [[Same-Content Internalist Accounts Strain Semantic Intuitions]] — Same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions.
- **Asserted (`P-60`):** [[Non-Millian Externalism Matches Internalist Differentiators]] — Non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism.
- **Negated (`~P-61`):** [[Narrow Syntactic Validity Explains Peter Rational Preparedness]] — It is not the case that a priori sensitivity to narrow syntactic validity independently explains slow-switched thinkers' rational preparedness.
- **Asserted (`P-67`):** [[Bridge - Comparative Superiority for Otero Reversal]] — If both (both (both (slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism) and (same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions)) and (non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism)) and (it is not the case that a priori sensitivity to narrow syntactic validity independently explains slow-switched thinkers' rational preparedness), then non-Millian content externalism has a strictly superior repertoire of replies to slow-switching puzzles compared to content internalism.
- **Negated (`~P-57`):** [[Content Externalism Has a Superior Repertoire of Replies on Peter]] — It is not the case that non-Millian content externalism has a strictly superior repertoire of replies to slow-switching puzzles compared to content internalism.

## Explanation

Peter's Earth/Twin-Earth switching puzzle poses an explanatory challenge for internalists just as much as for externalists. If internalist fixes that keep Peter's content the same across environments distort ordinary semantics, non-Millian externalism (which allows internal cognitive guises alongside external reference) can absorb every workable internalist distinction, and narrow formal syntax gives internalism no independent edge in explaining why Peter is rationally prepared to draw his conclusion, then externalism actually comes out strictly ahead on Peter's case. That comparative reversal directly collides with denying externalism's superiority over internalism.

## Usage in Literature

### Classical Argument

[[Manuel Pérez Otero]] (`A-03`; [[Otero 2012 - Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]], pp. 168–180) surveys every internalist strategy on Peter's `Pavarotti` inference—unified reference, disjunctive reference, three indexical strategies, and internally differentiated content—to reverse Boghossian's inference argument in favor of non-Millian externalism (`P-117`) and establish [[Content Externalism Has a Superior Repertoire of Replies on Peter]] (`P-57`):

1. Slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism.
2. Same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions.
3. Non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism.
4. It is not the case that a priori sensitivity to narrow syntactic validity independently explains slow-switched thinkers' rational preparedness.
5. If both (both (both (slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism) and (same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions)) and (non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism)) and (it is not the case that a priori sensitivity to narrow syntactic validity independently explains slow-switched thinkers' rational preparedness), then non-Millian content externalism has a strictly superior repertoire of replies to slow-switching puzzles compared to content internalism.
6. Both (slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism) and (same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions).
7. Both (both (slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism) and (same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions)) and (non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism).
8. Both (both (both (slow-switching puzzles arise neutrally for both content externalism and content internalism) and (same-content internalist accounts of slow switching strain standard semantic and ontological intuitions)) and (non-Millian content externalism incorporates every viable internal cognitive differentiator available to content internalism)) and (it is not the case that a priori sensitivity to narrow syntactic validity independently explains slow-switched thinkers' rational preparedness).
9. Therefore, non-Millian content externalism has a strictly superior repertoire of replies to slow-switching puzzles compared to content internalism.

### Premise Rejections and Replies

- **Rejecting `~P-61` (Narrow syntactic validity explains rational preparedness):** [[Paul Boghossian]] (`A-01`; [[Boghossian 2014 - Reply to Otero's Boghossian's Inference Argument against Content Externalism Reversed]], pp. 1–3) accepts theory-neutrality (`P-58`) and the comparative bridge (`P-67`), but rejects `~P-61` by arguing that Otero's taxonomy overlooks narrow syntactic individuation (`D-11`, `P-23`), on which Peter's inference is syntactically valid and directly explains his rational preparedness to draw the conclusion (`P-61`). Boghossian also contests Otero's focus on proper names (`Pavarotti`), arguing that general natural-kind terms like `water` cannot be excluded from rationality assessments and expose severe costs in externalism's anaphoric and logical-luck replies, thereby blocking `P-57`.
