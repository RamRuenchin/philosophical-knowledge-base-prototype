# Ruth Garrett Millikan (`A-11`)

Reconstructed via [[Szubka 2000 - Meaning Rationalism, A Priori, and Transparency of Content]]: endorses biosemantic content externalism (`P-40`) and rejects Strong Meaning Rationalism (`P-20`) and modest synchronic transparency (`P-19`) from the strict priority of the third-person viewpoint ([[Szubka 2000 - Meaning Rationalism, A Priori, and Transparency of Content#Propositions]]).
