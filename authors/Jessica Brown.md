## Other Attitudes
- **Conditional reductio assumption of Fregeanism:** Assumes [[Fregeanism About Mental Content]] (`P-43`) conditionally inside the Frances element case ([[Brown 2004 - Anti-Individualism and Knowledge#Arguments]]) to demonstrate that even neo-Fregean anti-individualism fails [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`) (`TDC`), motivating her rejection of [[Fregeanism About Mental Content]] (`P-43`) and [[Mixed Internal-External Content Externalism]] (`P-117`) in favor of symmetric non-Fregean wide externalism ([[Pure Wide Realist Content Externalism]] (`P-116`), [[Non-Fregean Wide Content Individuation Definition]] (`D-36`)).
- **Defeasible empirical challenge to transparency:** Cites Wason-task reasoning fallacies and Mates puzzles in [[Brown 2004 - Anti-Individualism and Knowledge]] as independent defeasible evidence against [[Rational Inference Requires Comparative A Priori Transparency]] (`P-13`).
