## Other Attitudes
- **Conditional / reductio assumptions:** Assumes [[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`) and [[Two-Concept Content Externalism]] (`P-41`) conditionally inside incompatibility arguments ([[Slow-Switching Equivocation Derivation]] (`ARG-01`), [[Pierre and Paderewski Opacity vs. Transparency of Sameness]] (`ARG-04`)) to derive failures of [[Transparency of Sameness of Content]] (`P-10`), [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`), [[Token-Level Wide APLA Holds]] (`P-21`), and [[Wide Content Alone Suffices for Rationalizing Explanation]] (`P-54`) rather than endorsing pure wide externalism.
- **Dialectical critique of contextualist replies:** Rejects Stalnaker's Ship/Peter tacit identity presupposition strategy as circular in [[Boghossian 2011 - The transparency of mental content revisited#Arguments]].
- **Diachronic trajectory (1992–2014):** Maintains comparative transparency ([[Rational Inference Requires Comparative A Priori Transparency]] (`P-13`)) and non-wide rationalizing explanation ([[Rationalizing Explanation Requires a Non-Wide Intentional Level]] (`P-55`)) throughout, while in 2014 conceding that Peter's puzzle is theory-neutral ([[Peter Case Is Theory-Neutral Across Content Theories]] (`P-58`)) and sharpening his narrow-syntactic APLA reply ([[Narrow-Syntactic APLA Holds]] (`P-23`), [[Narrow Syntactic Validity Explains Peter Rational Preparedness]] (`P-61`)) against Otero ([[Otero Comparative Reversal vs. Boghossian Narrow-Syntax Reply]] (`ARG-07`)).
