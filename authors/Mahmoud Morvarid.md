# Mahmoud Morvarid (`A-08`)

Critiques traditional privileged-access routes to CE–JFA incompatibility and develops a new reliability-based transparency argument showing that two-concept externalism (`P-41`) undermines Transparency of Sameness (`P-10`) and thereby conflicts with [[Accessibilist Justification Internalism]] (`P-119`, specifically `JFA`) ([[Morvarid 2021 - A new argument for the incompatibility of content externalism with justification internalism#Arguments]]).

## Other Attitudes
- **Conditional / plausible assumption:** Treats [[Two-Concept Content Externalism]] (`P-41`) and [[Accessibilist Justification Internalism]] (`P-119`, formulated as J-Factor Accessibility) as conditional assumptions to derive the incompatibility of two-concept externalism with [[Transparency of Sameness of Content]] (`P-10`) (`TSC`) and `P-119` in [[Morvarid 2021 - A new argument for the incompatibility of content externalism with justification internalism#Arguments]].
- **Dialectical critique of alternative routes:** Rejects traditional privileged-access incompatibility arguments (using Oscar's token J-factor `H`, in [[Morvarid 2021 - A new argument for the incompatibility of content externalism with justification internalism#Arguments]]) and critiques Jessica Brown's non-Fregean coriander/cilantro argument ([[Pierre and Paderewski Opacity vs. Transparency of Sameness]] (`ARG-04`)) for assuming unnoticed contradiction automatically lowers justification.
