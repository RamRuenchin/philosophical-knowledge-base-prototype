# Brie Gertler (`A-16`)

Disambiguates transparency in rational inference: endorses first-order rational sensitivity (`D-06`, `P-14`) while rejecting reflective explicatory access (`P-15`) via Shoemaker's Rita case and defending narrow Fregean cognitive value (`D-34`) via Burge's Bert differential dubitability argument ([[Gertler 2015 - Internalism, Externalism, and Accessibilism#Arguments]]).

## Endorses
- [[First-Order Rational Sensitivity Definition]] (`D-06`)
- [[Narrow Fregean Cognitive Value Definition]] (`D-34`)
- [[Rational Inference Requires Transparency of Mental Content]] (`P-01`)
- [[Rational Inference Requires First-Order Rational Sensitivity]] (`P-14`)
- [[Single-Token Content Infallibility Holds]] (`P-17`)
- [[Externalism Is Compatible with Knowledge of Content Proper]] (`P-30`)

## Rejects
- [[Accessibilist Justification Internalism]] (`P-119`)
- [[Rational Inference Requires Reflective Explicatory Access]] (`P-15`)
- [[Public Linguistic Standards Partly Determine Cognitive Value]] (`P-79`)

## Other Attitudes
- **Adaptation of mentalist internalism:** Draws on Feldman and Conee's distinction between [[Mentalist Justification Internalism]] (`P-120`) and [[Accessibilist Justification Internalism]] (`P-119`) to reject accessibilism while modeling narrow internalism about [[Cognitive Value]] on internal supervenience without reflective access ([[Gertler 2015 - Internalism, Externalism, and Accessibilism]]).
- **Dialectical neutrality on higher-order transparency:** Concedes privileged self-knowledge via content inheritance ([[Single-Token Content Infallibility Holds]] (`P-17`), [[Externalism Is Compatible with Knowledge of Content Proper]] (`P-30`)) and treats higher-order comparative transparency ([[Comparative A Priori Transparency Definition]] (`D-01`)) as orthogonal to cognitive value, focusing the internalism-externalism debate on first-order rational sensitivity ([[Rational Inference Requires First-Order Rational Sensitivity]] (`P-14`), Rita case) and narrow cognitive value ([[Narrow Fregean Cognitive Value Definition]] (`D-34`), Bert differential dubitability argument in [[Gertler 2015 - Internalism, Externalism, and Accessibilism#Arguments]]).
