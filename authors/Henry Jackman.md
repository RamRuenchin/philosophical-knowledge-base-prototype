# Henry Jackman (`A-07`)

Defends speaker-weighting metasemantic contextualism (`P-32`) and contextualist content externalism (`P-118`) via the Self-Interpretation Principle, arguing that single-context commitment weighting forces univocality in slow-switched Peter ([[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge#Arguments]]).

## Endorses
- [[Contextualist Content Externalism]] (`P-118`)
- [[Metasemantic Contextualism Adequately Explains Mental Content]] (`P-06`)
- [[Speaker-Weighting Metasemantic Contextualism Holds]] (`P-32`)
- [[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`)
- [[Boghossian Peter Slow-Switching Setup Possibility]] (`P-46`)

## Rejects
- [[Peter Commits Undetectable Equivocation]] (`P-47`)
- [[Radical Interpreter-Relative Contextualism Holds]] (`P-33`)
- [[Source-Dependent Contextualism Holds]] (`P-34`)

## Other Attitudes
- **Plausible self-knowledge preservation:** Treats [[Externalism Is Compatible with Knowledge of Content Proper]] (`P-30`) (Knowledge of Content Proper) as plausible and grounded in speaker authority via his Self-Interpretation Principle and variable commitment weights (detailed in [[Jackman 2015 - Externalism, metasemantic contextualism, and self-knowledge#Arguments]]).
- **Dialectical stance on switching:** Rejects [[Peter Commits Undetectable Equivocation]] (`P-47`) via single-context speaker weighting ([[Speaker-Weighting Metasemantic Contextualism Holds]] (`P-32`)) while distinguishing his view from both radical interpreter-relativity ([[Radical Interpreter-Relative Contextualism Holds]] (`P-33`)) and source-dependent contextualism ([[Source-Dependent Contextualism Holds]] (`P-34`)).
