# Sanford C. Goldberg (`A-06`)

Synchronically consistent across 1999, 2007, and 2008: accepts anti-individualism (`P-40`) and two-concept switching (`D-21`), rejects Discriminatory Knowledge of Content (`D-26`, `P-31`) and Transparency of Difference (`P-11`) while analyzing their downstream impact on discursive justification and cognitive value ([[Goldberg 1999 - The Relevance of Discriminatory Knowledge of Content#Arguments]], [[Goldberg-2007-Anti-Individualism, Content Preservation, and Discursive Justification#Arguments]], [[Goldberg-2008-Must Differences in Cognitive Value be Transparent#Arguments]]).

## Endorses
- [[Pure Wide Realist Content Externalism]] (`P-116`)
- [[Two-Concept Originary Fixation Memory Definition]] (`D-21`)
- [[Discriminatory Knowledge of Content Definition]] (`D-26`)
- [[Social-Externalist Public Cognitive Value Definition]] (`D-35`)
- [[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`)
- [[Two-Concept Content Externalism]] (`P-41`)
- [[Univocality of Referential Intent Secures Deductive Validity]] (`P-56`)
- [[Boghossian Peter Slow-Switching Setup Possibility]] (`P-46`)
- [[Public Linguistic Standards Partly Determine Cognitive Value]] (`P-79`)

## Rejects
- [[Content Externalism Is Compatible with Introspective Knowledge of Content]] (`P-05`)
- [[Externalism Is Compatible with Discriminatory Knowledge of Content]] (`P-31`)
- [[Transparency of Difference of Content]] (`P-11`)

## Other Attitudes
- **Dialectical grant:** Grants [[Externalism Is Compatible with Knowledge of Content Proper]] (`P-30`) (Knowledge of Content Proper) as plausible for argument's sake in [[Goldberg 1999 - The Relevance of Discriminatory Knowledge of Content#Arguments]] to show that failures of discriminatory knowledge of content ([[Externalism Is Compatible with Discriminatory Knowledge of Content]] (`P-31`)) still matter to truth-directed reasoning (Sally's 'pragmatist' case).
- **Conditional engagement with univocality:** In [[Goldberg-2007-Anti-Individualism, Content Preservation, and Discursive Justification#Arguments]], grants that [[Univocality of Referential Intent Secures Deductive Validity]] (`P-56`) and [[Anaphoric Univocality-Preserving Memory Definition]] (`D-22`) can block Boghossian's deductive equivocation, in order to prove his dilemma (`I1` vs. `I2`) refuting reflectively computable discursive justification.
- **Synchronically consistent stance (1999–2008):** Consistently defends anti-individualism ([[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`), [[Two-Concept Content Externalism]] (`P-41`)) while accepting revisionary externalist consequences for discursive justification ([[Goldberg-2007-Anti-Individualism, Content Preservation, and Discursive Justification#Arguments]]) and public cognitive value ([[Public Linguistic Standards Partly Determine Cognitive Value]] (`P-79`), Schmidt case in [[Goldberg-2008-Must Differences in Cognitive Value be Transparent#Arguments]]).
