# Roberto Horácio de Sá Pereira (`A-17`)

Endorses global content externalism (strengthening `P-40`) and derives that reasoning is both formally and materially individuated—opposing pure reasoning-transparency (`P-16`)—while defending dual internal-blamelessness and social-material rationality (`D-17`, `P-29`) ([[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons#Arguments]]).

- [[Reasoning-Transparency Definition]] (`D-08`)
- [[Dual Internal-Blamelessness and Social-Material Rationality Definition]] (`D-17`)
- [[Content Externalism Is Compatible with Rational Inference]] (`P-04`)
- [[Externalism Is Compatible with Dual Social-Material Rationality]] (`P-29`)
- [[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`)

## Rejects
- [[Rational Inference Requires Transparency of Mental Content]] (`P-01`)
- [[Rational Inference Requires Reasoning-Transparency]] (`P-16`)

## Other Attitudes
- **Radicalized global externalism (in individual summary):** Strengthens [[Content Externalism Holds]] (`P-40`) to global content externalism to derive material individuation of reasoning (opposing [[Rational Inference Requires Reasoning-Transparency]] (`P-16`)) in [[de Sá Pereira 2019 - Dangerous Liaisons#Arguments]], leaving only abstract formal abilities internal.
